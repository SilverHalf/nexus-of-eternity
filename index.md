## Composition Notes

Most groups seem to be progressing with a similar composition:

- Heal or Celestial <img class='inline troubadour'> Troubadours. Utilities: <img class='inline feedback'> Feedback, <img class='inline rogue'> Tale of the Honorable Rogue, <img class='inline marshal'> Tale of the Valiant Marshal.
- Heal <img class='inline paragon'> Paragons running ???
- BoonDPS <img class='inline specter'> Specter or <img class='inline luminary'> Luminary. Specter runs double venoms and <img class='inline smoke-screen'> Smoke Screen, spear secondary for additional CC.
- Possibly toad evoker can be used for more DPS at the cost of CC.
- DPS are usually <img class='inline scourge'> Scourges or condition <img class='inline weaver'> Weavers. Scourges are running <img class='inline breach'> Ghastly Breach for extra boonstrip.

General tips:
- Run trailblazer gear when possible for extra survivability, there is a lot of incoming damage.
- Ranged DPS is extremely important as it is difficult to stay in melee range and the fight requires constant movement.

## Mechanics Information

---

### <img class='inline empowered'> Empowered

Each stack increases outgoing damage by 1% and reduces incoming damage by 1%.

- Vloxx gains 10 <img class='inline empowered'> stacks after each CC at 70%, 40% and 10%.
- Champions grant Vloxx a <img class='inline empowered'> stack 24s after they spawn and then every further 20s they are alive.
- Vloxx gains <img class='inline might'> periodically at 5 <img class='inline empowered'> stacks. At 15 <img class='inline empowered'> this becomes 25 <img class='inline might'>; at 50 <img class='inline empowered'> additionally gains <img class='inline resolution'> and at 75 <img class='inline empowered'> gains <img class='inline protection'>.

---

### Add Behaviour

There are three adds:
- The *Staff* spawns at the beginning of the fight.
- The *Spear* spawns at the 70% split.
- The *Sword* spawns at the 40% split.

Spawn positions are constant.

Additionally:
- Each add will respawn 40 seconds after being killed.
- Each add gains a defiance bar when hitting 25% HP and drops 3 orbs when that bar is broken or when it's killed. - Adds cannot give orbs twice
- After 24 / 44 / 64 / 84 / ... seconds of being alive, each add will give the boss one stack of <img class='inline empowered'>.
- Projectile denial against staff projectile effects causes projectiles to bounce off and deal large AoE damage.

---

### Fixation

Selects one player randomly. Can be reset with stealth.

Will always select the closest person if nobody else is in sight: unreliable and impractical everywhere except for the very beginning of the fight.

Has a maximum range (between 2-3k?).

Most attacks will focus the fixated player, most noticeably:
- The boss will dash towards them
- They will always gain a green
- They will always gain a spread

---

### Worldbreaker

Attack that starts appearing from p2 onwards. Spawns six red arrows that [down? kill?] players. The arrows then become walls that inflict <img class='inline knockback'> and high <img class='inline burning'> to anyone close to them.

It is possible to walk through them with <img class='inline stability'>. It also does not extend through the boss's hitbox, meaning you can walk through it with no problem.

It is difficult to stay melee with them active: it's very easy to get too close and take excessive damage. This is why most groups will play ranged builds.

---

### Greens

Target the fixate and the two closest players: try to bait them.

Require at least three players in each. Failing them results in stacks of <img class='inline empowered'> and downstates the group.

Greens cannot be stacked or distorted.

## Strategy

---

### Phase 1

Trigger the fight by sending in only one person. This will result in only one green spawning at the start.

Play it much the same as in normal mode. *DO NOT* use reflects in this phase: if you reflect the staff's projectile attack, it bounces off and results in several very high damage AoEs.

Stunbreak chains with <img class='inline marshal'>. You *cannot* stack chains: have two players in each.

Sort out groups for greens. Usually commander tag + two markers. Green solving can be done by eye (there is plenty of time) or with standard anchor + runner + backup strategy like in ToF.

Red AoE under the boss after it moves will corrupt boons.

Try to cleave down the staff by standing opposite it from the boss. It will run in to around 300-400 range. Send a player to collect orbs afterwards if you have any stacks on the boss.

---

### 70% Split Phase

CC and Kill the champion Piercer to unlock Vloxx's defiance bar. If you cc it too slowly it will port away.

---

### Phase 2

The boss starts summoning in spears that fall down, targeting all players and leaving lingering AoEs. These can be extremely dangerous, similar to Boneskinner hands. Set up a reflect rotation using at least 3 reflect skills to avoid them, or be ready to move constantly.

The fixated player must stand always on the inside of the arena compared to the boss: otherwise it can dash out of bounds, and even fall off the platform.

---

### 40% Split Phase

CC and Kill the champion Bulwark to unlock Vloxx's defiance bar. If you cc it too slowly it will port away.

---

### Phase 3

AoE hell. Try to constantly move to avoid damage. Stack pools together to save space.

---

### 10% Split Phase

CC and Kill the champion Sunderer to unlock Vloxx's defiance bar. If you cc it too slowly it will port away.

---

### Final Phase

Try to kill as many champion weapons as possible while Vloxx transitions, prioritizing staffs.

Keep rotating reflects when you see spears like in phase 2.

Keep moving out of AoEs and placing pools far from the boss like in phase 3.
